title: "Varicella and Herpes Zoster"
---

We provide infectious disease modelling of varicella (chickenpox) and herpes zoster (shingles) in Norway, with a focus on the epidemiological and health economic evaluation of potential vaccination programmes, as part of a wider working group at NIPH.

Our work is described in the report [Epidemiological modelling and health economic evaluation of vaccination programmes against varicella and herpes zoster in Norway](https://www.fhi.no/en/publ/2026/epidemiological-modelling-and-health-economic-evaluation-of-vaccination-programmes-against-varicella-and-herpes-zoster-in-norway/) published by the Norwegian Institute of Public Health (FHI) in 2026.
